Newsflash · PHP security · October 22, 2026

Does your website use PHP? Check its version now.

CERT-FR reports two critical vulnerabilities that allow an attacker to execute code remotely. Patches have been available since October 21. If your installation is affected, updating it is urgent.

Read the CERT-FR alert and check the affected versions

What’s the problem?

An explainer video is coming to YouTube.

Remote code execution allows an attacker to run their instructions on a server. To find out whether your project is affected, you need to check its PHP version and environment. The CERT-FR bulletin lists the affected versions and patches.

An alert shared across four countries

National agencies have published their guidance. In the Netherlands, the NCSC even reports temporarily taking its own website offline as a precaution.

Summaries of official publications dated October 21, 2026, consulted on October 22. Each card links to the original source.

France · CERT-FR / ANSSI

cert.cyber.gouv.fr · October 21, 2026

Alert and recommendations

Isolate affected systems and apply patches

CERT-FR recommends isolating affected systems, then migrating to a supported version with the latest patches. Its bulletin confirms that patches are available.

Japan · IPA

ipa.go.jp · October 21, 2026

Early warning and preparation

Identify where PHP is used to prepare for the update

Published before the patches were released, the IPA’s early warning asks organisations to identify their use of PHP and prepare to apply updates promptly when available.

Read the IPA’s early warning (Japanese) ↗

United Kingdom · NCSC

ncsc.gov.uk · October 21, 2026

Patching guidance

Prioritise updates to internet-facing systems

The NCSC asks organisations to identify PHP systems and install a patched version. It recommends considering taking systems offline if neither updates nor temporary mitigations can be applied quickly enough.

A few answers before we start

You might be
wondering…

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Explain the context, what should work and what you’ve tried in your own words. I lead the technical work; you contribute information, feedback and validation of the result.

What do we prepare during the free call?

These 20 minutes help us understand your situation and prepare for Vibe Pompier or Vibe Doctor’s diagnosis and ongoing support. We review the project and the access needed, then agree on a proposal before any commitment. Booking this call does not order a paid service.

Are fixes included in Vibe Pompier?

Yes, Vibe Pompier includes fixes for the agreed blocker. Together, we define the expected result and how to check it. If your request needs deeper analysis or a broader redesign, I explain this and propose an appropriate scope before you commit.

Can you work with my tool or technology?

We review your project, its complexity and the access needed before committing. This lets us agree on an intervention suited to your environment and my scope of work.

What is delivered?

The agreed fix, an explanation of the changes and the information you need to continue. We check the affected user journey together. Production deployment or a ready-to-deploy handover is specified in the proposal.

What price and timeline should I expect?

We agree on the deliverable, price, start date and schedule before beginning. You can decide based on a clear proposal, suited to the blocker and the value of resolving it.

When should I choose Vibe Doctor?

Vibe Pompier addresses a specific blocker in an existing project. Vibe Doctor helps you understand needs and possibilities, choose priorities, plan and track implementation over time, with advice and coaching. Useful applications of AI are among the opportunities to explore. You make the final decisions; technical interventions are scoped before commitment.
